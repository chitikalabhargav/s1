Seven Wonders of the World

  1. Great Wall of China

    The Great Wall of China is the longest man-made structure in the world.


  2. Petra (Jordan)

    Petra is an ancient city famous for its beautiful rock-cut buildings.


  3. Christ the Redeemer (Brazil)

    Christ the Redeemer is a giant statue that overlooks Rio de Janeiro.


  4. Machu Picchu (Peru)

    Machu Picchu is an ancient Inca city located high in the Andes Mountains.


  5. Chichen Itza (Mexico)

    Chichen Itza is a famous Mayan pyramid and archaeological site.


  6. Colosseum (Italy)

    The Colosseum is a historic Roman amphitheater used for gladiator contests.


  7. Taj Mahal (India)

    The Taj Mahal is a beautiful white marble monument built in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.
